# Results

This directory contains the principal analysis outputs generated by the cell–cell communication workflow in human lupus nephritis.

The results were generated using **CellChat** applied to the AMP Lupus Nephritis single-cell RNA-seq dataset (SDY997), with particular emphasis on communication involving **inflammatory macrophages**.

The outputs progress from global ligand–receptor inference to macrophage-centered interactions and pathway-level communication analysis.

# Result Files

## `All_CellChat_Interactions.csv`

Complete table of significant ligand–receptor interactions inferred by CellChat across the analyzed immune and stromal cell populations.

This table represents the global communication landscape and serves as the foundation for downstream macrophage-centered analyses.

Typical information includes:

- sender cell population
- receiver cell population
- ligand
- receptor
- signaling pathway
- inferred communication probability

---

## `Inflammatory_Macrophage_Communication_Table.csv`

Subset of inferred communication interactions involving inflammatory macrophages.

This table was used to investigate the role of inflammatory macrophages as signaling hubs within the lupus nephritis kidney microenvironment.

It captures macrophage-associated communication with immune and stromal populations.

---

## `Macrophage_to_CD4T_Interactions.csv`

Ligand–receptor interactions inferred from inflammatory macrophages toward CD4 T cells.

This output supports investigation of macrophage-derived signals potentially influencing helper T-cell activation and inflammatory responses.

---

## `Macrophage_to_CD8T_Interactions.csv`

Ligand–receptor interactions inferred from inflammatory macrophages toward cytotoxic CD8 T cells.

This analysis highlights potential macrophage-mediated regulation of cytotoxic immune programs.

---

## `Macrophage_to_Bcell_Interactions.csv`

Ligand–receptor interactions inferred from inflammatory macrophages toward B cells.

Of particular translational interest are signaling programs associated with B-cell activation and survival, including **BAFF-related communication**.

---

## `Macrophage_to_cDC2_Interactions.csv`

Ligand–receptor interactions inferred from inflammatory macrophages toward conventional dendritic cells (cDC2).

These interactions provide insight into communication between major myeloid populations within the inflammatory kidney microenvironment.

---

## `Macrophage_Pathway_Summary.csv`

Pathway-level summary of communication involving inflammatory macrophages.

Individual ligand–receptor interactions are aggregated into broader signaling programs to identify pathways potentially involved in macrophage-mediated immune regulation.

Examples of pathways identified during the analysis include:

- MHC-I
- MHC-II
- GALECTIN
- CCL
- TNF
- BAFF
- CLEC
- MIF
- ICAM

---

## `Pathway_Communication_Strength.csv`

Ranking of signaling pathways according to their inferred communication strength.

This table provides a systems-level view of the dominant signaling programs operating within the lupus nephritis kidney microenvironment.

Highly represented pathways include programs associated with:

- antigen presentation
- immune-cell recruitment
- innate immune activation
- lymphocyte regulation
- inflammatory signaling

# Key Findings

The CellChat analysis revealed a highly interconnected immune communication network within the lupus nephritis kidney.

Inflammatory macrophages emerged as prominent participants in the communication landscape, interacting with multiple immune and stromal populations.

Major signaling programs included:

| Biological Program | Representative Pathways |
|---------------------|-------------------------|
| Antigen presentation | MHC-I, MHC-II |
| Myeloid / innate signaling | CLEC, MIF |
| Leukocyte recruitment | CCL |
| Inflammatory signaling | TNF |
| B-cell regulation | BAFF |
| Immune regulation | GALECTIN |
| Cell adhesion | ICAM |

These results suggest that inflammatory macrophages may contribute to lupus nephritis not only through their intrinsic transcriptional state but also through extensive communication with other components of the renal immune microenvironment.

---

# Translational Interpretation

Several identified communication pathways have direct or potential therapeutic relevance.

**BAFF signaling** is particularly notable because the BAFF pathway is already therapeutically targeted in systemic lupus erythematosus and lupus nephritis.

Other macrophage-associated pathways, including **CCL, TNF, GALECTIN, and MIF signaling**, provide candidate communication programs for further mechanistic and therapeutic investigation.

These results complement the therapeutic target discovery performed in Project 1 by adding a network-level perspective: rather than considering candidate genes independently, this analysis evaluates how inflammatory macrophages participate in coordinated intercellular signaling programs.

# Reproducibility

All result tables are generated programmatically by the scripts available in the `scripts/` directory.

The general analysis sequence is:

```text
01_setup_cellchat.R
        │
        ▼
02_cellchat_human_amp_ln.R
        │
        ▼
CellChat Interaction Inference
        │
        ▼
Macrophage-centered Analysis
        │
        ▼
Pathway-level Results
        │
        ▼
Figures and Result Tables
```

No manual modification of result tables is required for the analysis workflow.

Large serialized R objects may be excluded from the repository because they can be regenerated from the source data and analysis scripts.
